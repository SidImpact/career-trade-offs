# 📖 User Guide

Welcome to **Career Trade-Offs**! This guide will help you navigate the platform to make informed decisions about your professional future.

## 1. Taking the Career Quiz
If you aren't sure where to start, click **Take the Quiz** in the top navigation bar. 
- You will be asked a series of questions regarding your tolerance for physical strain, mental stress, financial instability, and long-term commitment.
- Based on your answers, our matching algorithm will suggest the top 3 careers that fit your unique "struggle profile."

## 2. Exploring Careers
Navigate to the **Explore Careers** page to browse our database.
- **Search & Filter:** Use the search bar to find specific roles or filter by industry (e.g., Technology, Healthcare, Creative).
- **Sort:** You can sort careers by "Highest Job Growth", "Peak Salary", or "Safest AI Risk".
- **Dynamic AI Generation:** If you search for a highly niche career (e.g., "Esports Gamer" or "Deep Sea Welder") and it doesn't exist in our database, simply click the **Generate AI** button inside the search bar. Our AI engine will build a complete, realistic trade-off profile for that career in seconds.

## 3. Reading a Career Profile
When you click on a career, you'll see a detailed breakdown:
- **Upsides:** The best parts of the job.
- **The Hidden Cost:** The brutal, unglamorous reality of the daily grind.
- **Trade-Off Profile:** A 1-5 rating system grading the financial, physical, mental, and social friction of the role.
- **Financial Runway:** Expected starting and peak salaries, plus the time it takes to see your first real paycheck.
- **Market Outlook:** Estimated 10-year job growth and AI displacement risk.

## 4. Comparing Careers
Torn between two paths (e.g., Software Engineer vs. Investment Banker)? 
- Go to the **Compare** tab.
- Search and add up to 3 careers to your comparison board.
- The platform will generate a side-by-side **Radar Chart** so you can visually compare the mental toll versus financial reward of each path.

## 5. Sharing Profiles
Every career profile has dedicated Social Sharing (OpenGraph) metadata. If you copy the URL of a career and paste it into iMessage, Twitter/X, or Discord, it will generate a beautiful preview card automatically!
